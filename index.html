Monster Encounter & Loot

Loot Inspection: DC, then the player's total

Coins come from each monster: about 10 gp (CR 0-4), 90 gp (CR 5-10), 1,100 gp (CR 11-16) and 9,000 gp (CR 17+) on an average roll. Tiers 3 and 4 are estimates. Below the DC pays a quarter. At the DC: average coins and 1-2 gems. DC+5 or more: double coins, more gems, potions and standard gear. The scale multiplies all coins.

Loot is tracked per monster. Once it is shown to a player it is gone, so a later, higher inspection roll only reveals what is additional. "Show total loot" shows the whole haul found so far.

Initiative

Forging an encounter adds the monsters and every online hero. Heroes roll their own initiative from their sheet. Players see names, AC and HP for heroes, and only a health status for monsters.

Found Loot (Outside Combat)

Uses the Party Level above. With a check total, the Loot Inspection DC decides the haul (below the DC is small, at the DC is average, DC+5 is rich). Leave it blank for a random result that fits the source. Chests vary widely in value; pick pockets stay small.
